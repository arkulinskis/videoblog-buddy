Everything a screen recorder should do

And the parts after recording that usually take all evening.

Screen + camera bubble

Record a tab, a window or the whole screen with your face in the corner. The camera is saved separately, so you can move or hide the bubble later.

Teleprompter

Write the script once. It scrolls in the recorder and in a floating window that stays on top of the tab you're showing.

Zooms on your clicks

With the browser extension, every click is remembered and the editor zooms in on it smoothly. Demos people can actually read.

Remove pauses

One button finds the silences and cuts them, with a little air left around your words. Undo is always there.

Captions + edit by words

Speech-to-text runs on your computer. Cut a sentence by selecting its words, remove every “um” in one click, download the .srt.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Recording, editing, exporting and the publish kit are free.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on a computer. The optional Chrome extension adds one-click tab recording and zooms on your clicks.

Where are my videos stored?

In your browser, on your computer. They are never uploaded to us. Your account keeps the plan: titles, scripts, edit decisions and descriptions. Download finished videos to keep them safe.

Does it upload to YouTube for me?

Yes. Sign in with Google once and upload the video, its thumbnail and captions straight from the publish kit. For Instagram or TikTok, export the MP4 and paste the caption the app wrote.

Are the captions sent to a server to be written?

No. Speech recognition runs in your browser (the model downloads once). Only the words are saved with your take, like your script.

Can I make Shorts and Reels?

Yes. Pick 9:16 in the editor or press "Make a Short version" on any video. The screen goes on top and your camera below.

Can I use it on my phone?

You can plan, write scripts, use the full-screen teleprompter and record camera takes. Screen recording needs a computer.

Where does the music come from?

Pick one of the Buddy beats the app makes for you (free to use, even on monetized videos), or bring your own audio file you have the rights to use.
