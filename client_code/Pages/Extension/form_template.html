VideoBlog Buddy for Chrome

Optional. The app records fine without it. With it, your demos look a lot more pro.

Add to Chrome: it's free or download the zip
Download the extension

One-click tab recording

On the tab you want to show, click the extension and press Record this tab. No picker, no hunting for the right tab.

Your clicks become zooms

Every click in the recorded tab is saved with its position, and the editor zooms in on it smoothly, like the pros do.

Click rings + key badges

A red ring where you click and a badge for shortcuts like Ctrl+K, right on the page, so viewers can follow.

Draw on the page

Alt+Shift+D, then drag to circle things on any website. The ink fades after a few seconds.

Shortcuts from any tab

Alt+Shift+R stops, Alt+Shift+P pauses, Alt+Shift+M drops a chapter marker, without switching tabs.

Private by design

It only talks to this site after you connect it, only while you record, and never sends what you type.

Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera)

  1. Press Add to Chrome above, then Add extension in the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Pin it: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to VideoBlog Buddy.
  3. Come back to this page, click the red VideoBlog Buddy icon and press Connect this site.
  1. Download the extension (button above) and unzip it. You get a folder called videoblog-buddy-extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin it: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to VideoBlog Buddy.
  5. Come back to this page, click the red VideoBlog Buddy icon and press Connect this site.

Firefox and Safari: the app records without the extension (choose a tab in the browser's share dialog). Click zooms and one-click recording need a Chromium browser.

Keyboard shortcuts

Stop recordingAlt+Shift+R
Pause / resumeAlt+Shift+P
Chapter markerAlt+Shift+M
Draw on the pageAlt+Shift+D

Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

What it can see

It runs on the pages you visit so it can draw click rings there. While you record, it sends this site the position of each click (not what you clicked on), and shows shortcut keys on the page. It never reads or sends the letters you type, page content, or your history. Nothing goes anywhere but this site, and only after you connect it.
